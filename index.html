About

Tommaso Pigliacampo

MSc Student in Economic and Social Sciences · Bocconi University

I study economics at Bocconi University. My research interests primarily span OTC markets, market microstructure, and information economics. Additional interests include market design, energy economics, industrial organization, and political economics. My current work focuses on electricity market design, long-term contracting, and policy options for renewable-dominated systems.
